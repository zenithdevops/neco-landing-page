Products

Three polymers. One purpose.

We specialise in the three plastics that matter most in everyday packaging, and return them to the production cycle.

1

PETPolyethylene terephthalate

Clear, strong and fully recyclable. We collect and process PET bottles and packaging into clean material ready for a new production cycle.

  • Beverage bottles
  • Food containers
  • Packaging
2

HDPEHigh-density polyethylene

Tough and chemical-resistant. Rigid HDPE is sorted, shredded and prepared for reuse in pipes, containers and household goods.

  • Milk & detergent bottles
  • Canisters
  • Crates
5

PPPolypropylene

Lightweight and heat-resistant. We recover polypropylene from caps, tubs and industrial parts and return it to manufacturing.

  • Bottle caps
  • Food tubs
  • Industrial parts

About

Waste is a resource.

I am Varodjan Abramian, founder and CEO of Necopolymer LLC.

For years I have worked to keep plastic out of Armenia's landfills and return it to industry as a valuable raw material.

At Necopolymer we collect, sort and process PET, HDPE and polypropylene at our facility in Kotayk Province, just outside Yerevan.

Our goal is simple: a cleaner country, and dependable recycled material for the businesses that build it.

Varodjan AbramianFounder & CEO, Necopolymer LLC
